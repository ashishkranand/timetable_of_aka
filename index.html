Reminder settings

Each class gets its own separate notification.

When enabled, Chrome will show an individual notification 15 minutes before each class. The page must remain open and your device must be awake. For reliable notifications, open this website through HTTPS (for example, after hosting it) rather than directly as a local file.

A static HTML page cannot send notifications while it is fully closed. If you want reminders when Chrome is closed, that requires a hosted installable app with a service worker and push-notification setup.
Your timetable is saved in this browser. Completion marks do not sync to other devices.
